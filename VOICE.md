## Claim

Prints of the age of sail, from the clipper lithographs to the 1930 J-class summer, each one naming its vessel, its artist or firm, and the water it sailed.

## Who reads it

A sailor, a yacht-club member, someone with a harbour in their family. They know the boats better than we do. They want the picture, a size that fits a wall, a price, and a person to write to about framing.

## Modes

- **The folio (invitation).** Hero, doors, sizes, the Folio, the Burgee. Second person, short. Nautical words only where a sailor would use them; never a sea metaphor on a price or an error.
- **The log (credential).** Collection notes, piece stories, the Craft essay, About. The vessel and the painter are the subject: Lane, Buttersworth, Jacobsen, the 1851 schooner, Shamrock V. Dates and waters exact.
- **Caption.** The holding institution, then the record link.
