## Left alone on purpose

- Every rights, marks, publicity and archive sentence on About and the 1930 wing, apart from the banned verb noted above: "public domain in the United States as of January 2026", "verified at acquisition", "sign-off-or-shelve", the marks doctrine and the footer fine print.
- The 184 piece stories in `data/catalog.json` (generated by `merge.mjs` from `data/sweep/`): object register, specific and sourced.
- Prices, tier names, THE FLEET at $13,500 and the Burgee at $1,400.
- The Burgee page's honesty rule and lint error: already name the cause and the rule.
- The craft essay's first three sections: specific and true.
- Clippers blurb "the den's oldest ocean": unclear but not false; left for the operator.

Every "Send the enquiry" click goes nowhere. The reply promise is cut; the address is left in place because choosing a new one is your call.
