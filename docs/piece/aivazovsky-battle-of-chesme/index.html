The Battle of Chesme at Night

WHATThe Turkish fleet burning at anchor by night, 1770 — fire, moon, and water sharing the light.

WHYAivazovsky painted the Russian navy's proudest night as an inferno reflected twice over.

NOTICEThe MOON keeps its own column of light apart from the fires.

“Victory at night is lit by the loser.”
