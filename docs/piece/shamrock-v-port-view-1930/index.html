The 1930 Season · J-Class 1930

Racing yacht Shamrock V, full, port, Aug. 16, 1930

WHATLipton's fifth and last challenger, full and by on the port tack, 16 August 1930 — the first J-class summer.

WHYAn anonymous press plate of the first challenger built to the new Universal Rule J class.

NOTICEThe BERMUDAN mainsail — one triangle where the Edwardian boats carried four sides and a topsail.
