The Hudson River Steamboat Rip van Winkle

WHATA Hudson River sidewheeler in strict profile, walking beam and paddle box drawn like an engineer's elevation.

WHYBard painted nearly every steamboat on the Hudson, port side, flags flying; builders were said to check his pictures against their plans.

NOTICEEverything is PROFILE — Bard barely ever turned a boat, because profile is where the measurements live.

“Measured with a brush.”
